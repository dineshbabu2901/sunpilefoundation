BORED DIRECT MUD CIRCULATION (DMC) PILES .

Bored piles installed with direct mud circulation for spoil removal.

SERVICE 02 / 04

ABOUT THE METHOD

Direct Mud Circulation (DMC) piling uses drilling fluid circulation during boring to help carry excavated material from the bore. The exact construction sequence, reinforcement and concreting method depend on the design and ground conditions.

Typical scope discussion

  • Review soil conditions, bore details and water/slurry management requirements.
  • Plan rig access, circulation equipment, work areas and spoil handling.
  • Discuss bore formation, casing or slurry support when required.
  • Coordinate reinforcement placement, concreting and applicable checks.

Where it may be considered

  • Bored pile works where direct mud circulation is considered suitable.
  • Sites requiring planning for slurry handling and drilling-fluid management.
  • Projects designed by a qualified geotechnical or structural engineer.

Important: This is general method information, not a design recommendation. The appropriate pile foundation and any testing procedure must be specified and reviewed by the project’s qualified engineer.
